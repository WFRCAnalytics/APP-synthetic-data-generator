# Slice 33: A column's settings in four groups

The right-hand panel of Review replaces "Advanced settings" with four collapsible groups, in the order a person thinks. Each has a title
and a one-line summary that stays visible when it is closed, so the panel reads as an outline.

1. **How the app read this column.** Type, date pattern, coordinate, and one block "Missing and special values" that shows what was
   found (blank rows, marked words, special codes) beside the two inputs: "Words that also mean missing" and "Special answer codes".
   Opens by itself when the column needs a look.
2. **What the fake column keeps.** The choices for its kind (counts, range, shape, missing values), with "Group very small labels as
   Other", "Hide label names" and "Make the average and spread match exactly" under what they affect. Always open.
3. **How it is written in the fake file.** "Name in the fake file", whether it is included, and the Excel format for a workbook.
4. **Connect to another table.** IDs from another table, row counts, a followed value. Shown only where it can apply.

The header carries the column name, its type and an "Include this column" switch; a column left out greys the groups below it. Which
groups a person opened or closed is kept for each column while they look at other columns, and is not stored in the browser.
